FORX Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 29, 2026

FORX Race ("FORX", "we", "us") is a local-first race tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app uses, what leaves the device, and the controls available to you.

Data FORX Uses On Device

FORX may ask permission to read Apple Health workout, heart rate, and heart rate variability data. FORX may also ask permission to write completed race workouts to Apple Health when you choose to save them.

Health data is used for race tracking and optional on-device summaries. FORX does not send HealthKit data to FORX servers, analytics payloads, crash logs, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Race history, station timing, preferences, onboarding state, and local app settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, race history may sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. FORX does not operate its own sync server and does not receive your private iCloud data through its own server.

Subscriptions

FORX PRO subscriptions are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. FORX uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether PRO features should be unlocked.

RevenueCat may process purchase history for app functionality and subscription analytics. FORX does not receive your credit card number or Apple payment credentials.

Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Tracking

FORX does not use advertising tracking, IDFA, data brokers, or cross-app tracking. Advertising personalization signals are explicitly disabled.

FORX uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to collect anonymous usage data and crash diagnostics. These SDKs may collect device model, OS version, app version, session duration, screen views, in-app events, and crash stack traces. This data is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID. HealthKit values, race times, station splits, workout routes, heart rate, HRV, sender IDs, and Watch sync payload internals are never included in analytics or crash payloads.

You can turn off Anonymous Analytics and Crash Reports individually in FORX → Profile → Privacy. Disabling crash reports takes full effect after restarting the app. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Firebase: policies.google.com/privacy.

FORX uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When analytics is enabled, an anonymised RevenueCat attribution signal is shared with Firebase to help understand subscription conversion. No personally identifiable information is included.

This website (forxrace.com) uses Google Analytics to understand visitor traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as pages visited, referral source, device/browser type, and approximate location. This is separate from the FORX app's in-app analytics settings and applies only to browsing this website. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

AI Features

AI insights are optional. When supported, AI summaries are intended to run on device. If AI is off or unavailable, FORX uses local rule-based insights and template summaries. FORX AI copy is not medical advice and should not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Your Controls

You can manage Health permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings. You can disable iCloud sync in your Apple account settings. You can turn Anonymous Analytics and Crash Reports on or off in FORX → Profile. You can turn AI insights on or off inside FORX Profile settings. You can manage or cancel FORX PRO subscriptions from your Apple account subscription settings.

For privacy or support requests, contact support@forxrace.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as FORX changes. The latest version should always be available on this page.
