Privacy, in plain language.

FORX is a local-first race tracker. This policy explains what the app uses, what can leave your device, and the controls available to you.

Data FORX uses on device

FORX may ask permission to read Apple Health workout, heart rate, and heart rate variability data. FORX may also ask permission to write completed race workouts to Apple Health when you choose to save them there.

Health data is used for race tracking and optional on-device summaries. FORX does not send HealthKit data to FORX servers, analytics payloads, crash logs, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Race history, segment timing, preferences, onboarding state, and local app settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, race history may sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. FORX does not operate its own sync server and does not receive your private iCloud data through its own server.

Subscriptions

FORX PRO subscriptions are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. FORX uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether PRO features should be unlocked.

RevenueCat may process purchase history for app functionality and subscription analytics. FORX does not receive your credit card number or Apple payment credentials.

Analytics, crash reporting, and tracking

FORX does not use advertising tracking, IDFA, data brokers, or cross-app tracking.

When Anonymous Analytics is enabled, FORX uses Firebase Analytics to measure allowlisted screen views, app lifecycle events, and feature interactions. Firebase assigns an app-instance identifier for aggregate usage measurement. FORX does not set a Firebase user ID or associate analytics with your name, email address, Apple account, advertising identifier, or a FORX account.

When Crash Reports is enabled, FORX uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash stack traces, relevant application state, app version, device and operating-system details, and privacy-filtered breadcrumbs immediately before a crash. FORX does not attach a user ID or free-form user content to crash reports.

The app enforces allowlists for analytics events, crash keys, crash messages, and crash metadata. HealthKit values, raw Health samples, race times, segment or station splits, workout routes, heart rate, HRV, notes, sender IDs, and Watch sync payload internals are not included in analytics or crash payloads.

If Crash Reports is disabled, Crashlytics does not transmit new reports. Crash information may remain locally on the device and can be sent if reporting is enabled again. Disabling Crash Reports fully applies after FORX is restarted.

This website

FORX uses Google Analytics on forxrace.com only after you choose “Allow analytics.” If you do not allow it, the analytics script is not loaded. When allowed, Google Analytics may collect pages visited, referral source, device or browser type, and approximate location.

Your website analytics choice is stored locally in your browser. It is separate from the FORX app’s Anonymous Analytics setting. You can reopen Cookie settings from the website footer at any time.

Google’s privacy policy applies to data processed by Google Analytics: policies.google.com/privacy.

AI features

AI insights are optional. When supported, AI summaries are intended to run on device. If AI is off or unavailable, FORX uses local rule-based insights and template summaries. FORX AI copy is not medical advice and should not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Your controls

  • Manage Health permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings.
  • Disable iCloud sync in your Apple account settings.
  • Independently disable Anonymous Analytics, Crash Reports, and AI insights inside FORX Settings.
  • Manage or cancel FORX PRO subscriptions from your Apple account subscription settings.
  • Use Cookie settings in this website’s footer to change the website analytics choice.

For privacy or support requests, contact support@forxrace.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as FORX changes. The latest version will remain available on this page.
