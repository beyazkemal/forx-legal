FORX Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

FORX Race ("FORX", "we", "us") is a local-first race tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app uses, what leaves the device, and the controls available to you.

Data FORX Uses On Device

FORX may ask permission to read Apple Health workout, heart rate, heart rate variability, and sleep data. FORX may also ask permission to write completed race workouts to Apple Health when you choose to save them.

Health data is used for race tracking, recovery-aware insights, and optional on-device summaries. FORX does not send HealthKit data to FORX servers, analytics payloads, crash logs, advertising networks, or data brokers.

Race history, station timing, preferences, onboarding state, and local app settings are stored on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, race history may sync through the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. FORX does not operate a custom sync server for this MVP and does not receive your private iCloud data through its own server.

Subscriptions

FORX PRO subscriptions are processed through Apple In-App Purchase. FORX uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether PRO features should be unlocked.

RevenueCat may process purchase history for app functionality and subscription analytics. FORX does not receive your credit card number or Apple payment credentials.

Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Tracking

FORX does not use advertising tracking, IDFA, data brokers, or cross-app tracking.

The app includes privacy guardrails for telemetry and crash metadata. HealthKit values, raw Health samples, station splits, workout routes, heart rate, HRV, sleep data, sender IDs, and watch sync payload internals must not be included in analytics or crash payloads.

If FORX adds a third-party analytics or crash SDK in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before release.

AI Features

AI insights are optional. When supported, AI summaries are intended to run on device. If AI is off or unavailable, FORX uses local rule-based insights and template summaries. FORX AI copy is not medical advice and should not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Your Controls

You can manage Health permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings. You can disable iCloud sync in your Apple account settings. You can turn AI insights on or off inside FORX Profile settings. You can manage or cancel FORX PRO subscriptions from your Apple account subscription settings.

For privacy or support requests, contact support@forxrace.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as FORX changes. The latest version should always be available on this page.
