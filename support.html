How can we help?

For direct support, email support@forxrace.com. Include your iPhone and Watch models, OS versions, and FORX version when relevant—never include Health values or race splits.

Common actions

Health access

Manage permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings. FORX’s timer, segment results, and local history still work when Health access is denied.

Apple Watch

Keep the iPhone and Watch paired and nearby. Open FORX on both devices before the session if race state is not visible on the Watch.

iCloud sync

Sign in to iCloud and keep iCloud Drive or CloudKit access enabled. FORX remains usable in local mode when iCloud is unavailable.

FORX PRO

Manage or cancel subscriptions from Apple account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases inside FORX if an active subscription is not reflected.

Race history

Finishing a session automatically saves it to local History and opens the race summary. There is no separate save step.

Privacy controls

Anonymous Analytics, Crash Reports, and AI insights can be controlled independently inside FORX Settings.

Session types

  • Full Simulation: 8 Run, 8 Transition, and 8 Station segments.
  • Station Practice: one selected station with 1–10 attempts.
  • Custom Simulation: a segment sequence built from runs, transitions, and stations.

Privacy requests

FORX does not operate its own account or race-history server. Race history is stored locally and may sync through your private Apple iCloud account when enabled. You can delete local race history in the app and manage iCloud data through Apple account or device settings.
