FORX Support

For support, contact support@forxrace.com.

Common Actions

  • Health access: manage permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings.
  • iCloud sync: sign in to iCloud on the device and keep iCloud Drive/CloudKit access enabled. FORX remains usable in local mode when iCloud is unavailable.
  • FORX PRO: manage or cancel subscriptions from Apple account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases inside FORX if an active subscription is not reflected in the app.
  • AI insights: turn AI insights on or off from Profile settings.

Privacy Requests

FORX does not operate its own account or race-history server. Race history is stored locally and may sync through your private Apple iCloud account when enabled. You can delete local race history in the app and manage iCloud data through Apple account or device settings.
